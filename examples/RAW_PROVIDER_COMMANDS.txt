RAW provider command handling:

SESSION_BEGIN
    Save the current camera values.

SET_POSITION_RAW|x|y|z|fov|fromStart
    Write x/y/z directly.
    Do not calculate Right/Up in the CT.

ROTATE_YAW_RAW|delta
    Add delta directly to the raw yaw value.
    UE3 delta is in Rotator units.
    UE4 delta is in degrees.

SESSION_END
    Restore the saved camera values.
